# DHARA city exploration

Open the Windows Explorer build and choose WHAT-IF LAB. Adjust rain, wind, temperature and drainage, then press Walk above the city. After the five-second descent, click inside the city and use WASD to walk, Shift to move faster and right-drag to look around. Press 1 for a smooth return to the overview or Escape/Home to reset the camera.

Walking uses the existing procedural district. The purchased SimplePoly asset has not been imported: the supplied .meta files contain folder identifiers, not model geometry. The explorer runs with the existing shared environmental simulation; navigation does not change the selected simulation conditions.

Movement stays at 2.2 metres above the city origin, slides along building footprints with a 0.6 metre clearance, and stops at the district boundary. This is an outdoor inspection mode, without building interiors or stair climbing.
