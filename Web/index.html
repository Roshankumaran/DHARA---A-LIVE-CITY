PROVENANCE & LIMITATIONS

Model data, clearly labeled.

Weather and air quality

Data comes directly from Open-Meteo over HTTPS. Each group retains its valid time in IST and successful retrieval time in UTC. Missing values remain unavailable, failures retain same-city data, and responses older than the acceptance window are rejected.

Weather definitions ↗ · Air quality definitions ↗

Simulation scope

DHARA uses a generic procedural district and simplified, uncalibrated environmental rules. Simulated water storage, lighting and haze are illustrative outputs, not live sensors, a reconstruction of the selected city, or a calibrated flood forecast. IMD advisories are unavailable.

Weather data: Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0. Air-quality model: CAMS through Open-Meteo. No API secrets are required by this companion dashboard.
